Stories of care and understanding

Patient Stories: Mental Health Journeys

Every person’s experience is different. These easy-to-read stories explore common reasons people seek support and what a first step toward care may involve.

About these stories These first-person stories are illustrative composite scenarios created for education. They do not describe actual patients. The professional-perspective quote boxes contain illustrative general guidance, not quotations from real clinicians. Nothing here is a testimonial or a promise of treatment outcomes.
