Frequently asked questions

Important information about this online community service.

Is there a physical clinic to visit?

No. This page represents a virtual community eClinic and provider directory. It does not represent a physical clinic address.

Does submitting a WhatsApp message confirm my appointment?

No. It sends an appointment request. The clinician or coordinator must confirm availability and the appointment details.

Are all listed services available every day?

Availability varies by provider and must be confirmed directly. Only configured services and providers are displayed.

What should I do in an emergency?

For a life-threatening emergency, call 112 or attend the nearest emergency department. This eClinic is not an emergency response service.
