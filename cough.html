Clinical Decision Support

Acute Cough

Point-of-care assessment for adults with newly presenting cough. Designed around antimicrobial stewardship, pneumonia exclusion, airway disease recognition and rational symptomatic treatment.

Clinical use only. This tool supports—not replaces—clinical examination and physician judgment. It is not a prescription generator. Check allergies, pregnancy status, renal/hepatic function, drug interactions, local resistance patterns and the applicable institutional/state protocol before prescribing.
