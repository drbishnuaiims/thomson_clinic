About Dr. Anushree Mishra
Psychology with empathy

A career spanning academic psychology, psychotherapy, healthcare, emotional intelligence, corporate training and community mental wellness.

Dr. Anushree Mishra is an accomplished Consultant Psychologist, Therapist and Academician with over 30 years of professional experience across higher education, healthcare and corporate training.

At AIIMS Bhubaneswar, she has been engaged as a Consultant Psychologist and Visiting Faculty since 2014, providing psychological support, counselling and academic guidance to medical and nursing learners.

Her professional interests include Applied Psychology, Emotional Intelligence, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, anxiety management, emotional restructuring, psychological tool validation and community mental wellness.

Her therapeutic philosophy combines structured psychological approaches with empathy, mindfulness, resilience-building and holistic wellbeing practices.
