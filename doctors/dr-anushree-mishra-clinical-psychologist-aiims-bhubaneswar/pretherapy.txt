Pre-Therapy Assessment
A calm, evidence-informed starting point for psychological wellbeing. Please complete this confidential assessment before your first therapy appointment.
Important: This is a paid 30–45 minute pre-therapy assessment. The session will explore your presenting concerns, current symptoms, relevant clinical and psychosocial history, functional difficulties, previous treatment, and expectations from therapy. The purpose is to conduct a preliminary clinical assessment, understand your psychological needs, develop an initial clinical formulation, and determine the most appropriate therapy approach and treatment plan. Your appointment will be confirmed after successful completion and verification of the payment. This form is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or have an active plan to harm yourself or someone else, seek urgent in-person emergency care.
