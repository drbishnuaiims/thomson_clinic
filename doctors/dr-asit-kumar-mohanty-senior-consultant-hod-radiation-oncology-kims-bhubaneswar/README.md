About Dr Asit Kumar Mohanty
Radiation Oncologist

Dr. Asit Kumar Mohanty is a senior Radiation Oncologist with over 20 years of extensive clinical experience in the field of Radiation Oncology.

He is currently associated with KIMS Cancer Center as Senior Consultant and Head of Department – Radiation Oncology.
