About

Clinical background and approach to care

Dr. Asit Kumar Mohanty is a senior Radiation Oncologist with over 20 years of extensive clinical experience in the field of Radiation Oncology. He is currently associated with KIMS Cancer Center as Senior Consultant and Head of Department – Radiation Oncology. His professional experience includes work at prestigious institutions such as Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai, A.H. Regional Cancer Centre, Cuttack, and Kuwait Cancer Control Centre, Ministry of Health, Kuwait.

Approach to care
Multidisciplinary cancer care · Evidence-based treatment planning · Precision radiotherapy · Individualised oncology decisions
