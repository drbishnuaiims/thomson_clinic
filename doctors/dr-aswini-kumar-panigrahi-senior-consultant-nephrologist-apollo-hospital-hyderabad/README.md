About Dr. Panigrahi
Senior Nephrologist

MBBS, MD (Internal Medicine), DNB Nephrology with more than two decades of dedicated experience in nephrology.
Dr. Aswini Kumar Panigrahi is a senior nephrologist with over 23 years of dedicated experience in the field of nephrology.

His clinical practice encompasses comprehensive adult nephrology, including acute kidney injury, acute renal failure, chronic kidney disease and a broad range of complex renal disorders.

He is associated with Apollo Hospitals Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, and has a particular clinical focus on complex kidney disease and advanced renal care.

His professional experience also includes renal transplantation, with the attached profile documenting his role in the first ABO-incompatible renal transplant at Apollo, Hyderabad.
