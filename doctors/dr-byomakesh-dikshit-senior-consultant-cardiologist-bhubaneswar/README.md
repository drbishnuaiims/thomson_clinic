About the Cardiologist
Specialist cardiac care

Dr. Byomakesh Dikshit is listed as a Cardiologist practising at Apollo Hospitals in Gajapati Nagar, Bhubaneswar.

His documented qualifications progress from undergraduate medical training through postgraduate Medicine to super-speciality training in Cardiology.

He completed his MBBS from Sambalpur University in 1999, followed by MD – Medicine from VSS Medical College in 2008 and DM – Cardiology from SCB Medical College & Hospital in 2012.

The profile lists a range of cardiac diagnostic and treatment-related services, including Trans-esophageal ECHO, BP Monitoring, Colour Doppler, Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring, Holter Monitoring, Temporary Pacemaker, PAMI and Catheter Ablation.
