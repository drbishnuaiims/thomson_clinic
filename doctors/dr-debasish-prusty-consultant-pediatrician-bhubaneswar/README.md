About Dr Debasish Prusty
Consultant Pediatrician

Postgraduate training in Pediatrics with experience across government healthcare, hospital-based practice, academic medicine and research.

Dr Debasish Prusty is a Pediatrician with postgraduate training in Pediatrics and experience in government healthcare, hospital-based pediatric practice, academic medicine, research, and medical education. He provides comprehensive, evidence-based pediatric care ranging from newborns and infants to adolescents, with an emphasis on accurate diagnosis, rational treatment, preventive healthcare, developmental assessment, and family-centered care.

His clinical approach combines scientific evidence, careful clinical assessment, appropriate investigations, rational prescribing, preventive pediatrics, and clear communication with parents and caregivers. He believes that pediatric healthcare should address not only the immediate illness but also the child's growth, development, nutrition, emotional well-being, school performance, and long-term health.

Beyond clinical practice, Dr Prusty has a strong interest in academic pediatrics and research, with postgraduate research focused on childhood respiratory illness. He is committed to providing compassionate, ethical, child-friendly, and scientifically grounded medical care while avoiding unnecessary investigations and medications whenever they are not clinically indicated.
