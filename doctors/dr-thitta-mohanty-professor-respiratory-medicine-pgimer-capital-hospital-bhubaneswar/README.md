PROFESSIONAL PERSPECTIVE
From specialist chest care to departmental leadership.
Dr. Thitta Mohanty's career brings together respiratory medicine, tuberculosis care, procedural expertise, postgraduate education, institutional development and service to people who need access to respiratory care.
Dr. Thitta Mohanty is a Professor of Respiratory Medicine and Head of the Department of Respiratory Medicine at PGIMER and Capital Hospital, Bhubaneswar.

His professional journey has extended across district healthcare, Employees' State Insurance services, dedicated chest and tuberculosis services, medical college practice and tertiary respiratory medicine. Over the years, this experience has encompassed the assessment and management of respiratory disease, tuberculosis and drug-resistant tuberculosis, alongside specialist respiratory procedures.

His academic role has developed in parallel with clinical practice. Teaching, postgraduate training, departmental development and institutional responsibilities form an important part of his current professional position.

Alongside his institutional responsibilities, his social service initiative eNiswas reflects a commitment to extending respiratory care to people who may otherwise face barriers in accessing specialist healthcare.
