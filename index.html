The core of Thomson Clinic

A clinic built around the mind and brain.

Mental health and neuroscience are not simply one department within Thomson Clinic. They are the central clinical focus.

Psychiatry, psychology, therapy, sleep, addiction, child and adolescent mental health and neurobehavioral care are brought together into one specialized ecosystem.

Other medical specialties connect around this core when physical health, neurological or systemic conditions require specialist attention.

Care that fits real life

Different people. Different realities.

Mental healthcare should reach people where they actually live, study, work and grow.

Community & rural care

Mental healthcare should travel beyond the clinic.

Telepsychiatry and connected care can help extend specialist mental healthcare to communities that may otherwise have limited access.

Student mental health

Support for students.

Academic pressure, anxiety, sleep problems, attention difficulties and emotional wellbeing.

Online therapy

Care from your own space.

Remote consultations where clinically appropriate.

Built for India

Mental healthcare for real Indian lives.

From students navigating academic pressure to working adults, parents, older adults and people living in communities far from specialist centres, Thomson Clinic is designed around the diversity of how mental health is experienced across India.

The goal is not simply to move a consultation online. It is to make specialist care more accessible while keeping the clinical relationship human.
