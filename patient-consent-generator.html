Informed consent for consultation

This consent form is prepared with reference to the Government of India Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) and the Telepsychiatry Operational Guidelines (2020). It is a clinic documentation aid, not a government-issued form or a substitute for the treating professional’s duties. Complete the relevant fields and acknowledgements to generate a consent PDF. Review it in your browser before sharing it with Thomson Clinic through WhatsApp. If anything needs correction, return to the form and generate a fresh PDF.

01

Patient details

Fields marked * are required. Date and time are generated automatically.

For online consultations, provide the location where you will physically be during the session. This helps the clinician respond appropriately if an urgent situation arises.
Select the person who initiated the teleconsultation. If the patient initiated it, the patient’s name entered above will be reflected in the consent PDF.
If the patient is a minor or cannot legally provide consent, the clinician must follow the applicable legal framework for consent, capacity and any authorised representative. An adult patient may ordinarily participate in a private teleconsultation without a family member or informant; no female informant is required solely because the patient is female. For a minor, an adult family member should be present as required by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines. Where a patient may lack capacity to make the relevant healthcare decision, the clinician will assess capacity and follow the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, including applicable nominated-representative/caregiver provisions. A spouse or caregiver is not automatically a legal substitute decision-maker.
02

Consent checklist

Select the applicable acknowledgements below. Required items are marked *. Use the selection controls to select mandatory items or all visible checklist items.
Clinical and regulatory note: This form is a clinic documentation aid informed by India’s Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) and Telepsychiatry Operational Guidelines (2020). The RMP must independently verify identity, assess capacity and suitability, document implied or explicit consent as applicable, maintain records and arrange in-person or emergency referral when clinically indicated. The treating professional remains responsible for confirming suitability of teleconsultation, documenting the consultation, prescribing only as permitted, and arranging in-person/emergency referral when needed. The clinic should obtain legal and clinical review before deployment.
03

Photograph and signature

The PDF places the photo at the top, signature near the bottom and optional Aadhaar image below the signature.

Patient photograph (optional)

JPG or PNG; preferably under 5 MB.

Photograph of signature *

Upload a clear image of the patient's or authorised representative's signature; preferably under 5 MB.

Masked Aadhaar image (optional)

Upload a JPG or PNG image of the masked Aadhaar only. The image will appear in the generated PDF immediately below the signature section.

Optional. Upload a masked Aadhaar only. Do not upload an unmasked Aadhaar image. The image will be included in the PDF and visible to anyone who receives it.

Uploading a signature image is an electronic acknowledgement for this workflow; it does not by itself guarantee legal validity or identity verification.
04

Generate and share consent

Generate the PDF and review it in your browser. If anything needs correction, return to the form and generate a fresh consent PDF before sharing.

Privacy and data handling: This standalone form does not save form entries in browser storage, does not submit information to Thomson Clinic or any database, and does not send the entered details to a server. Entries and uploaded images, including any Aadhaar image, are held temporarily in the active page session only to generate the PDF locally. No appointment, consultation request or patient information is automatically submitted. The PDF is saved only when you choose to download it. The Aadhaar image, if uploaded, must be a masked Aadhaar and becomes part of the PDF. Review the PDF in the browser. If any correction is needed, return to the form and generate a fresh PDF. WhatsApp sharing is user-initiated; you must manually attach the downloaded PDF. The PDF library is loaded from a third-party CDN, which may receive ordinary connection metadata; form fields are not sent to that CDN.

Review your consent PDF

Check the document carefully before sharing. If you need to change anything, return to the form and generate a fresh PDF.

On supported mobile browsers, choose WhatsApp from the device share menu to share the PDF attachment. If file sharing is unavailable, the PDF will download and WhatsApp will open; attach the downloaded file manually.
