A little about me

Care that begins with listening.

Mental health concerns can affect how we think, feel, sleep, work and connect with the people around us. Seeking help is an important step towards understanding these experiences and finding a way forward.

My approach to psychiatry emphasises careful assessment, collaborative decision-making and treatment plans informed by clinical evidence and the person's individual needs. Each consultation is an opportunity to understand the concerns that matter most to the person seeking care.

At Thomson Clinic, the aim is to make professional mental healthcare more accessible while maintaining a thoughtful, respectful and confidential clinical environment.

The consultation experience

Clinical care, with you at the centre.

A good consultation is not just about identifying symptoms. It is about understanding the context, discussing options and agreeing on a practical plan.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with your concerns

Discuss your symptoms, history, current difficulties and what you hope to achieve from treatment.

02 / ASSESS

Build a clinical picture

Review relevant clinical information and consider possible explanations for the difficulties you describe.

03 / COLLABORATE

Discuss your options

Understand suitable treatment approaches, their potential benefits and limitations, and your preferences.

04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Plan the next steps

Agree on practical recommendations, follow-up arrangements and further assessment when needed.

Before your appointment

A few helpful answers.

Know what to expect before requesting a consultation.

Use the appointment request form on this page. Your preferred consultation format and contact details will help the clinic coordinate the request.

Consider bringing relevant previous prescriptions, medical reports, a list of current medicines and a brief account of your main concerns.

Online consultations may be suitable for many concerns. The clinician will determine whether remote assessment is appropriate or an in-person evaluation is needed.

Clinical information is handled in accordance with applicable professional and legal confidentiality requirements, subject to relevant exceptions. Please use a private setting for online consultations.
